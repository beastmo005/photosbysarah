About Sarah

Hi, I’m Sarah Ochu! I’m a photographer and a senior at Texas A&M University studying agricultural communications and journalism with a minor in hospitality. I love turning meaningful moments into photographs you can hold onto, whether that’s celebrating graduation, capturing a milestone or helping someone feel confident in front of the camera.

My interest in photography grew during four years of high school yearbook, where I learned to look for the expressions and small details that tell a bigger story. Since then, I’ve photographed friends and family and continued developing my skills through college coursework in photography, design and visual storytelling. Studying abroad in Italy gave me another opportunity to explore photography while experiencing new places and perspectives.

Agricultural communications brings together my interests in creativity, storytelling and connecting with people. It has helped me understand how words and images can make complex subjects approachable and share the stories behind agriculture, food and the communities they support. Through writing, design and photography projects, I’ve practiced creating work that is both engaging and purposeful.

I value kindness, clear communication and attention to detail. For me, a great photography experience begins with helping you feel comfortable, listening to your ideas and making room for your personality to shine.

As I prepare to graduate in December, I’m excited to grow my photography business and pursue a career that combines communications, creativity and event planning. Wherever that path leads, I want to help people tell their stories and celebrate the moments that matter.
